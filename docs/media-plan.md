# Media inventory and page plan — initial batch (25 November 2026)

Source: owner's local folder `CakeopediaMedia/batch1` (41 files: 7 videos, 34 images). Access to the laptop path was
verified. Originals were not modified. Private provenance (neutral slug to source file) is in the gitignored
`private/` folder; this page deliberately omits source filenames, absolute paths and customer names.

## How it was inspected
- Metadata with ffprobe (video) and sharp (image). Every image was viewed on a contact sheet; every video was reviewed as six
  frames spread across its duration. This is sampled review, not real-time viewing of every second.
- All videos are 2160x3840 portrait except one low-resolution 478x850 clip. Four videos have audio tracks; audio is
  stripped from every derivative.

## Held back (not used publicly)
- Legible customer or child names on cakes, backdrops or toppers (9 photos, 3 videos): need permission or a crop decision.
  One otherwise-good vintage heart cake was removed after its piped name proved legible.
- Licensed-character cakes (Frozen, Mickey) and one low-resolution clip with a burned-in Instagram handle.
- Cakes whose lettering could not be read reliably.
