Source: https://www.dshs.texas.gov/retail-food-establishments/permits-retail-food-establishments/texas-cottage-food-production (fetched directly; the earlier agent fetches failed on DNS). Everything below is quoted/paraphrased from that page only; the rest of this file is still from secondary sources. Not legal advice; owner and legal review required before anything is published.
- **SB 541:** allowable foods now include any food EXCEPT meat/poultry, seafood, ice and frozen desserts (ice cream, frozen custard, gelato, popsicles), low-acid canned goods, products with CBD/THC, raw milk products. Annual gross income threshold for a cottage food production operation (CFPO): **$150,000**.
- **Training:** the individual who operates the CFPO must complete an accredited basic food handler course (Health and Safety Code Ch. 438).
- **TCS foods are allowed but trigger duties:** a CFPO that sells time/temperature-control-for-safety foods **must register with DSHS** (Online Licensing Registry) and follow TCS labelling; TCS foods must be stored and delivered at 41 F or below (cold) / 135 F or above (hot). TCS foods cannot be wholesaled or donated. (Which Cakeopedia items are TCS, e.g. cream/custard fillings or Rasmalai, is an owner/DSHS question, not decided here.)
- **Registration is otherwise optional** and lets the label show a DSHS identification number instead of a home address.
- **Online sales are allowed if:** the consumer buys from the operator; the operator, an employee or a household member **personally delivers**; before accepting payment the website posts the required label information in a legible statement; after payment the label shows the address or DSHS number. (No shipping by third parties.)
- **Label must have:** legible writing; operation name; address or DSHS number; common name of the product; major allergens (eggs, tree nuts, soy, peanuts, milk, wheat, sesame) when used; the exact statement "THIS PRODUCT WAS PRODUCED IN A PRIVATE RESIDENCE THAT IS NOT SUBJECT TO GOVERNMENTAL LICENSING OR INSPECTION." Unpackaged items: the same information on an invoice or receipt. **TCS foods also need:** date made on the label, and "SAFE HANDLING INSTRUCTIONS: To prevent illness from bacteria, keep this food refrigerated or frozen until the food is prepared for consumption." in at least 12-point font (label, or invoice/receipt).
- **Local authorities** may not require a permit or fee from a CFPO; DSHS/local health can act on immediate serious threats (emergency/recall orders). Public health entities comply from 1 Sept 2025.
- **Website implication (draft, NOT published):** because ordering is online, the site must show the label information statement before payment (the private-residence statement and, if applicable, the allergen/safe-handling lines) and the owner decides between address and DSHS number on labels. Contact for questions: foodestablishments@dshs.texas.gov / 512-834-6753 (from the page).

## Where Cakeopedia's menu may touch the TCS question (questions for the owner, not findings)
Nothing here says an item is allowed or forbidden. These are the items that most plausibly need a ruling from DSHS or an adviser before they are sold or advertised:
1. **Rasmalai Cake** (rasmalai pieces in milk-based filling; also Rasmalai cupcake filling): dairy-based and moist.
2. **Lemon curd** (Lemon Blueberry Cake; lemon curd cupcake filling): egg-based.
3. **Swiss meringue buttercream** (most cakes and cupcakes): cooked egg whites and butter. Many guides treat buttercream differently from cream cheese or whipped cream; confirm.
4. **Chocolate ganache and truffle ganache**, **dulce de leche**, **butterscotch and caramel sauces** (cream or condensed milk). Confirm whether cream-based ganache counts.
5. **Berry and fruit fillings** (house-made berry filling, blueberry, strawberry cupcake fillings): water activity and acidity; confirm.
6. **Anything the owner refrigerates or advises customers to refrigerate**: the care page tells customers to "refrigerate as instructed", which suggests refrigeration matters for some cakes. If a product needs refrigeration for safety, it may be TCS.
7. **Delivery**: delivery in North Austin at extra cost, and Zelle advance payment, both need checking against the sales-channel and temperature rules, especially in summer heat.
8. **Sales volume**: ask whether last-12-month gross sales are anywhere near the cap.
9. **Selling cakes that go beyond baked goods**: fondant, acrylic toppers and artificial flowers are non-edible decorations; confirm no rule affects them.
